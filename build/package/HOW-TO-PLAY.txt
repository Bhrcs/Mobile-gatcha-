CINDERBOUND - HOW TO PLAY  (v0.5.0)
===================================

START
  Double-click Cinderbound.exe. No install, no Godot, no extra files needed.
  Windows SmartScreen may warn because the file is unsigned:
  choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".
  The game opens in a tall phone-shaped window. Resize or maximise it freely;
  the layout scales and stays sharp.
  Your old save loads automatically - nothing is lost (see SAVES).

THE LOOP
  Clear stages -> earn EXP, Gold, materials, Wisps and Gems -> train and evolve
  your heroes -> summon new heroes -> climb the towers -> push into new regions.
  Nothing can be bought with real money; everything is earned by playing.

GETTING AROUND
  Bottom bar: HOME - QUEST - UNITS - SUMMON - MENU.
  BACK (top left) always returns to the screen you came from, even after long
  detours (Units -> Details -> Evolution -> Where to find -> Tower -> BACK takes
  you straight back to Evolution). The "?" (top right) opens Help & Guide.
  PC keys: Esc = close a popup / go back (pause in battle), Enter = confirm.
  Hover (PC) or tap and hold (touch) icons, currencies and rewards for details.

TOP BAR (every screen)
  RANK + EXP bar (tap: PROFILE), ENERGY (current / max and the time to the next
  point - tap for details), GEMS and GOLD. Big numbers are shortened
  (12.4K, 1.3M); hover or hold for the exact value. Gains flash green, spending
  flashes red.

HOME
  Your squad around the campfire (tap a hero, tap again for a reaction), the big
  QUEST button, then UNITS and SUMMON, then TOWER, MISSIONS and SQUAD.
  Locked buttons show a padlock and say which stage opens them. Red markers mean
  something is waiting (a new hero, an evolution that is ready, rewards to claim).
  First-time guides point at the next thing to try.

MENU
  Squad, Items, Missions, Towers, Codex, Profile, Settings, Help & Guide and the
  title screen.

FEATURES UNLOCK AS YOU PLAY
  Clear 1-4   Auto Battle
  Clear 1-5   Units and a 5-hero Squad (+ a new hero joins you)
  Clear 1-6   Training (+ Wisps)
  Clear 1-7   Elemental Towers
  Clear 1-8   Evolution (+ Fragments)
  Clear 1-9   Summoning at the Embergate (+ 150 Gems)
  Clear 1-10  Missions and World 2: Saltglass Reach
  Each new feature is introduced with a short popup the first time.

QUEST
  The QUEST tab shows every region as a card: completion %, stars, whether its
  boss is defeated, or what opens it. The Elemental Towers card is here too.
  On a region's map the stage markers tell you what to expect:
    ring = new stage   diamond = elite foes   crest = boss
    ring + check = cleared   gold ring + check = all 3 stars   padlock = locked
  Tap a stage for its details: Energy cost, your Squad Power vs the recommended
  power, the three STAR objectives, POSSIBLE DROPS (hover / hold for each item)
  and the FIRST CLEAR reward. START opens PREPARE: your squad, the leader skill,
  and your squad's elements against the foes' elements with one line of advice.
  EDIT SQUAD if you like, then DEPART (this spends the Energy).
  Squad Power and element advice never stop you - you can always depart.

ENERGY
  Stages cost 2-5 Energy, tower floors 5-9. You regain 1 Energy every 3 minutes,
  also while the game is closed. Every Rank Up refills Energy completely, and Max
  Energy grows as your Rank rises. If you are short, a popup shows how long to wait.

BATTLE CONTROLS (touch or mouse)
  Choose target     tap an enemy on the field or its plate at the top
  Attack            TAP a hero card
  Burst             SWIPE UP on a hero card when its Burst gauge says READY
  Guard             SWIPE DOWN on a hero card (half damage until your next turn)
  AUTO              heroes act on their own (Burst when useful, Guard against
                    charged attacks). While AUTO is on, a glowing AUTO BATTLE plate
                    sits above the cards - tap it (or AUTO) to take over.
  1x / 2x           battle speed. Settings -> default speed / start on AUTO.
  MENU / Esc        pause: RESUME, ELEMENTS (the element chart), SETTINGS, RETREAT.
  Keyboard: A attack, B Burst, G guard, Tab / Left / Right change target, 1-5 hero.

BATTLE BASICS
  Elements: Fire beats Nature, Nature beats Water, Water beats Fire (+25% / -25%).
  Elements are shown by symbol (flame, drop, leaf) as well as colour.
  Statuses: Burn and Poison hurt each turn; Regen heals; ATK/DEF/REC Up and Down;
  Shields absorb damage; Provoke draws attacks. Hover or tap an icon to read it.
  Enemy roles: attackers, healers (defeat them first), tanks with shells, buffers,
  debuffers, and chargers. ELITE enemies wear a gold frame and know an extra trick.
  DANGER - GUARD!: the foe is charging a devastating attack - Guard, heal or
  finish it first. Boss plates show PHASE 1/2 and a mark on the HP bar where the
  boss changes phase.
  STARS: each stage has 3 objectives (clear it, no hero defeated, a turn limit or
  Burst count). Your best result per objective is kept.

RESULTS
  Victory reveals stars, EXP (heroes can gain several levels at once), Gold,
  items and first-clear rewards. Tap anywhere or SKIP to fast-forward; Enter
  continues. Defeat gives one tip for that fight and RETRY / EDIT SQUAD /
  STAGE SELECT.

HEROES
  12 heroes, 4 per element, rarity 3 to 5 stars. Each has a role, stats (HP, ATK,
  DEF, REC), a normal attack, a Burst, a Passive and a Leader Skill (works when the
  hero leads the squad - e.g. Kael: Fire allies +8% ATK).
  UNITS: quick element buttons plus FILTER & SORT (element, rarity, role, can
         evolve, favourites; sort by recent, level, rarity, power, HP, ATK, DEF,
         REC, name or element, high or low first). Your choice is remembered.
         Every card shows the same markers in the same places: squad/leader top
         left, NEW / EVOLVE / MAX top right, lock and favourite bottom left,
         rarity stars bottom right. CODEX shows every hero you have met.
  DETAILS: VIEW (full-size hero with animations), stats (the hero's best stat is
         marked), skills with short facts (targets, % ATK, hits, effects),
         passive, leader skill, Burst level, evolution path, LOCK / FAV, TRAIN,
         EVOLVE, SQUAD.
  Level caps: 3-star 15, 4-star 25, 5-star 35.

TRAINING
  TRAIN opens the Training screen: each Wisp card has -1 / +1 / +5 / MAX
  (Ember / Tide / Verdant give +50% to heroes of their element, Radiant Wisps work
  for everyone). The preview shows Lv now -> new Lv, the EXP bar, stat gains and
  the Gold cost before you confirm; nothing is spent until you press TRAIN.
  AUTO picks same-element Wisps first. Heroes also gain EXP from every won battle.

BURST LEVELS (1-5)
  +1 Burst EXP each time a hero uses Burst in a won battle, or train it with a
  Spark Sigil or 20 Soul Shards. Every Burst improves differently (more damage,
  stronger heals or shields, longer buffs, lower gauge cost...).

EVOLUTION
  At max level, EVOLVE shows the current form -> next form, the stat changes,
  the materials (owned / needed) and the Gold cost. WHERE TO FIND lists every
  stage, tower floor, mission or login day that gives a material, with GO buttons;
  BACK brings you right back to the evolution screen. One confirmation shows
  exactly what will be used, then EVOLUTION COMPLETE sums up what changed.
  The hero restarts at Lv.1 with a higher cap and keeps its Burst level.
  Evolution never needs duplicate heroes.

SUMMONING - THE EMBERGATE
  1 summon = 100 Gems, 10 summons = 1000 Gems (the cost is on each button).
  Rates: 3-star 75%, 4-star 22%, 5-star 3% (DETAILS lists every hero's chance).
  A hero you already own (any form) is marked DUPLICATE and becomes Soul Shards
  instead: 10 / 30 / 100. Tap any result to inspect it; NEW heroes can be added to
  the squad straight away. SKIP appears once you have watched your first summon.
  Results are saved before the animation plays.
  Gems: first clears, rank milestones (every 5 ranks), missions and login rewards.

TOWERS
  Ember, Tide and Verdant Tower, 10 floors each, climbed floor by floor along the
  tower's own path. Floors get harder and cost more Energy; floors 5 and 10 hold a
  guardian boss. Each tower is the best place to farm its element's Fragments,
  Cores and Crystals; floor 10 gives a Prism Shard (needed for 5-star evolutions).

MISSIONS & LOGIN
  Five daily missions (claim 4 to open the Daily Chest) and weekly missions, each
  with a progress bar. CLAIM ALL collects everything that is finished at once.
  The 7-day login calendar never resets - claimed days are ticked, a missed day
  simply continues where you left off.

ITEMS
  Tabs: MATERIALS, TRAINING, ITEMS, OTHER (Gems, Gold, Soul Shards, Energy).
  Tap an item: the panel below shows what it does, WHERE TO FIND, and USE for
  items you can use from here (Wisps -> pick a hero to train).

SETTINGS (MENU -> SETTINGS, also on the title screen and in the battle pause menu)
  AUDIO     Master / Music / Effects volume.
  GAMEPLAY  Default battle speed, start battles on AUTO, damage numbers,
            reset tutorial tips (vibration on phones).
  GRAPHICS  Fullscreen, screen shake, battle effects, reduce motion (no sliding
            menus), screen margins (extra space for notches / rounded corners).
  ACCOUNT   Name and profile, save info, title screen, version.

HELP & GUIDE
  MENU -> HELP, or the "?" on a screen: Basics, Battle, Elements (chart),
  Heroes and Progress in short entries.

SAVES
  Automatic. Stored in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Cinderbound\
  (cinderbound_save.json + .bak backup, cinderbound_settings.json for settings).
  Saves from v0.1-v0.4 load with all progress kept.
