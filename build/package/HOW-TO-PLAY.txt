CINDERBOUND - HOW TO PLAY  (v0.4.0)
===================================

START
  Double-click Cinderbound.exe. No install, no Godot, no extra files needed.
  Windows SmartScreen may warn because the file is unsigned:
  choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".
  The game opens in a tall phone-shaped window. Resize or maximise it freely;
  the layout scales and stays sharp.
  Your old save loads automatically - nothing is lost (see SAVES).

THE LOOP
  Clear stages -> earn EXP, Gold, materials, Wisps and Gems -> train and evolve
  your heroes -> summon new heroes -> climb the towers -> push into new regions.
  Nothing can be bought with real money; everything is earned by playing.

TOP BAR (every screen)
  RANK + EXP bar (tap it for your PROFILE), ENERGY (current / max and the time to
  the next point - tap it for details), GEMS, GOLD.

HOME
  Your squad around the campfire (tap them!), the big QUEST button and tiles for
  UNITS, SQUAD, SUMMON, TOWER, ITEMS and MISSIONS. Locked tiles show a padlock and
  say which stage opens them. Red markers mean something is waiting for you
  (a new hero, an evolution that is ready, rewards to claim, an unseen Embergate).
  Bottom bar: HOME - UNITS - QUEST - SUMMON - MENU.
  MENU: Squad, Items, Missions, Codex, Towers, Profile, Settings, Title screen.

FEATURES UNLOCK AS YOU PLAY
  Clear 1-4   Auto Battle
  Clear 1-5   Units and a 5-hero Squad (+ a new hero joins you)
  Clear 1-6   Training (+ Wisps)
  Clear 1-7   Elemental Towers
  Clear 1-8   Evolution (+ Fragments)
  Clear 1-9   Summoning at the Embergate (+ 150 Gems)
  Clear 1-10  Missions and World 2: Saltglass Reach
  Each new feature is introduced with a short popup the first time.

QUEST
  Tabs at the top switch regions (1 Ashroot Wilds, 2 Saltglass Reach).
  Tap a stage for its details: Energy cost, your Squad Power vs the recommended
  power, the three STAR objectives, POSSIBLE DROPS and the FIRST CLEAR reward
  (5 Gems, 25 on boss stages). START opens PREPARE: check the squad and the
  leader skill, EDIT SQUAD if you like, then DEPART (this spends the Energy).
  Squad Power is only advice - you can always depart.
  Cleared stages can be replayed as often as you like.

ENERGY
  Stages cost 2-5 Energy, tower floors 5-9. You regain 1 Energy every 3 minutes,
  also while the game is closed. Every Rank Up refills Energy completely, and Max
  Energy grows as your Rank rises. If you are short, a popup shows how long to wait.

BATTLE CONTROLS (touch or mouse)
  Choose target     tap an enemy on the field or its plate at the top
  Attack            TAP a hero card
  Burst             SWIPE UP on a hero card when its Burst gauge says READY
  Guard             SWIPE DOWN on a hero card (half damage until your next turn)
  AUTO              heroes act on their own (Burst when useful, Guard against
                    charged attacks). Tap a card or AUTO again to take over.
  1x / 2x           battle speed. Settings -> default speed / start on Auto.
  Keyboard: A attack, B Burst, G guard, Tab / Left / Right change target, 1-5 hero.

BATTLE BASICS
  Elements: Fire beats Nature, Nature beats Water, Water beats Fire (+25% / -25%).
  Statuses: Burn and Poison hurt each turn; Regen heals; ATK/DEF/REC Up and Down;
  Shields absorb damage; Provoke draws attacks. Tap and hold an icon to read it.
  Enemy roles: attackers, healers (defeat them first), tanks with shells, buffers,
  debuffers, and chargers. ELITE enemies wear a gold frame and know an extra trick.
  WARNING / CHARGING: the enemy's next move is a devastating attack - Guard, heal
  or finish it first. Bosses have patterns, call minions and enrage below half HP.
  STARS: each stage has 3 objectives (clear it, no hero defeated, a turn limit or
  Burst count). Your best result per objective is kept.

HEROES
  12 heroes, 4 per element, rarity 3 to 5 stars. Each has a role, stats (HP, ATK,
  DEF, REC), a normal attack, a Burst, a Passive and a Leader Skill (works when the
  hero leads the squad - e.g. Kael: Fire allies +8% ATK).
  UNITS: filter by element, sort (recent, power, level, rarity, element, favourite),
         CODEX shows every hero you have met (unknown heroes are silhouettes).
  DETAILS: stats and power, passive, leader skill, Burst level, evolution path,
         LOCK / FAV, TRAIN, EVOLVE, SQUAD.
  Level caps: 3-star 15, 4-star 25, 5-star 35.

TRAINING
  TRAIN: choose Wisps (Ember / Tide / Verdant give +50% to heroes of their element,
  Radiant Wisps work for everyone). The preview shows Lv now -> new Lv, stat gains
  and the Gold cost before you confirm. AUTO SELECT fills the level for you.
  Heroes also gain EXP from every won battle.

BURST LEVELS (1-5)
  +1 Burst EXP each time a hero uses Burst in a won battle, or train it with a
  Spark Sigil or 20 Soul Shards. Every Burst improves differently (more damage,
  stronger heals or shields, longer buffs, lower gauge cost...).

EVOLUTION
  At max level, EVOLVE shows the current form -> next form, the stat changes,
  the materials (owned / needed) and the Gold cost. Each material has OBTAINED FROM
  with GO buttons that take you straight to a stage or tower floor that drops it.
  The hero restarts at Lv.1 with a higher cap and keeps its Burst level.
  Evolution never needs duplicate heroes.

SUMMONING - THE EMBERGATE
  1 summon = 100 Gems, 10 summons = 1000 Gems. Rates: 3-star 75%, 4-star 22%,
  5-star 3% (DETAILS lists every hero and its exact chance).
  A hero you already own (any form) becomes Soul Shards instead: 10 / 30 / 100.
  After the ceremony: DETAILS, ADD TO SQUAD or CONTINUE. SKIP appears once you have
  watched your first summon. Results are saved before the animation plays.
  Gems: first clears, rank milestones (every 5 ranks), missions and login rewards.

TOWERS
  Ember, Tide and Verdant Tower, 10 floors each. Floors unlock one by one, get
  harder and cost more Energy; floors 5 and 10 hold a guardian boss. Each tower is
  the best place to farm its element's Fragments, Cores and Crystals; floor 10
  gives a Prism Shard (needed for 5-star evolutions).

MISSIONS & LOGIN
  Five daily missions (claim 4 to open the Daily Chest) and weekly missions.
  The 7-day login calendar never resets - a missed day simply continues where you
  left off.

ITEMS
  Tabs: MATERIALS, TRAINING, ITEMS, OTHER (Gems, Gold, Soul Shards, Energy).
  Items stack. Tap an item to see where it is obtained.

SETTINGS
  Volumes, Screen Shake, Battle Effects, Damage Numbers, Fullscreen,
  Start Battles on Auto, Default Battle Speed, Reset Tutorial.

SAVES
  Automatic. Stored in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Cinderbound\
  (cinderbound_save.json + .bak backup). Saves from v0.1-v0.3 are upgraded
  automatically: heroes, levels, gold, cleared stages and tutorial progress are
  kept; old elemental shards become Fragments; heroes above the new level cap are
  set to max level (ready to evolve); milestone gifts you already earned are given.
